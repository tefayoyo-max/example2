FAQ

  • What file formats are accepted? — Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, and PDF.
  • How is the compliance score calculated? — Based on submitted vs. expected reports ratio.
  • Can I edit a submitted report? — Yes, admins can re-upload and re-verify.
  • How do I add a new establishment? — Go to Admin → Establishments → New Establishment.
